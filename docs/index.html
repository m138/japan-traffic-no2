NO2: annual mean nitrogen dioxide (µg/m³, 1 ppb = 1.88 µg/m³), estimated for each fiscal year from the Road Traffic Census, monitoring stations and satellite data. Near-road contribution: the part of NO2 explained by census roads within 1 km. Nighttime noise: outdoor Lnight (22:00–06:00, 4 m) from census roads within 200 m with 2021 traffic. High co-exposure: Lnight ≥ 55 dB and NO2 ≥ 20 µg/m³ (FY2022). Local roads and screening by buildings are not included. The values are for comparing areas, not for single homes.
Source: D. Matsushita, “Diverging trends in nitrogen dioxide and nighttime road traffic noise in Japanese cities” (submitted). Code: github.com/m138/japan-traffic-no2 (MIT). Boundaries: 2020 Population Census small areas (e-Stat), generalized. Map lines delineate study areas and do not necessarily depict accepted national boundaries.
