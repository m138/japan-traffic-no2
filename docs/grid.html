Nitrogen dioxide in Japan by grid cell, fiscal years 2015–2023

Population-weighted means of 12.3 million residential points in grid cells of about 0.4 km

2022
Map by municipality

Point to a cell

Move the pointer over the map, or tap it, to see the values of a cell.

NO2. Annual mean nitrogen dioxide (µg/m³; 1 ppb = 1.88 µg/m³), estimated for each fiscal year from the Road Traffic Census, monitoring stations and satellite data. The estimates are made at residential points, which are random points inside each census small area, about one per 10 residents. A cell shows the population-weighted mean of its points. The color scale is the same for all years.

Change in NO2. NO2 of fiscal year 2023 minus NO2 of fiscal year 2015. The estimate has a lower bound of 0.94 µg/m³ (0.5 ppb), and its error in cross-validation is about 2 ppb (3.8 µg/m³). Some cells in areas with low NO2 show a small increase that is within this error. At the monitoring stations with data for both years, NO2 rose at 11 of 1,449. Increases of more than 4 µg/m³ are rare (404 cells). Some of them follow expressway sections that are new in the 2021 traffic census, and the others are in areas with low NO2, where the estimate is uncertain.

High co-exposure. Share of the residents of a cell with Lnight ≥ 55 dB and NO2 ≥ 20 µg/m³ (fiscal year 2022). Lnight is the outdoor nighttime noise (22:00–06:00, 4 m) from census roads within 200 m with 2021 traffic. Local roads and screening by buildings are not included.

Reading the map. Cells are 0.004° (about 0.4 km). Coarser cells are shown at small scales. Cells without residents are not colored, and gray cells have few residents. Outside cities a cell often holds only one or two points, so the value of a single cell is uncertain. The values are for comparing areas, not for single homes.

Source: D. Matsushita, “Diverging trends in nitrogen dioxide and nighttime road traffic noise in Japanese cities” (submitted). Code: github.com/m138/japan-traffic-no2 (MIT). Background map: GSI Tiles (Geospatial Information Authority of Japan). A grid map of nighttime noise is in a related project.
