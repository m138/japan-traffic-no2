# Data sources and terms of use

The input data are not included in this repository (except the emission factor table in `inputs/`). The terms below were checked on 2026-11-15; check them again before reuse.

| Data | Source | Terms | Credit line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Road Traffic Census 2015 and 2021, section lines (`traffic_census_2015_converted.parquet`, `traffic_census_2021_converted.parquet`) | GitHub shiwaku/mlit-road-traffic-census-converter, release data-v1 | Converter code and data: MIT. Original data: Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT), Public Data License 1.0 | Source: MLIT, Road Traffic Census (FY2015, FY2021), processed |
| 2020 census small-area boundaries with population | e-Stat (Statistics Bureau of Japan) | Government of Japan Standard Terms of Use 2.0 (compatible with CC BY 4.0) | Source: e-Stat, 2020 Population Census small-area boundary data, processed |
| Air quality monitoring, annual values and station metadata (FY2015–2023) | National Institute for Environmental Studies (NIES), Environmental Numerical Database, via tenbou.nies.go.jp | Free to use with credit | Source: NIES Environmental Numerical Database (air quality monitoring data of the Ministry of the Environment), processed |
| Road traffic noise monitoring (FY2015–2023) | Ministry of the Environment, via NIES Environmental GIS+ | Free to use with credit | Source: Ministry of the Environment, results of continuous monitoring of road traffic noise (via NIES Environmental GIS+), processed |
| TROPOMI-inferred surface NO2, Asia 2019 | Cooper et al. (2022), Zenodo record 5484305 | As stated on the Zenodo record | Cooper, M.J. et al. Global fine-scale changes in ambient NO2 during COVID-19 lockdowns. Nature 601, 380–387 (2022) |
| NOx emission factors | National Institute for Land and Infrastructure Management (NILIM), Technical Note No. 671 (2012), Table 6.4 | Coefficients transcribed from a public report | Source: NILIM Technical Note No. 671 |
| Secondary medical areas | National Land Numerical Information A38 (MLIT) | MLIT terms for National Land Numerical Information | Source: MLIT National Land Numerical Information (medical areas), processed |
| External comparison: Anenberg et al. (2022) | figshare 12968114 | As stated on figshare | Anenberg, S.C. et al. Lancet Planet. Health 6, e49–e58 (2022) |
